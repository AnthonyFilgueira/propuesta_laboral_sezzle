    Idea to production: FlowPay for Cambios Jotta

    A Peru–Venezuela remittance business ran every transfer by hand, over chat. Not my business, but the whole idea came from studying the owner's workflow: I designed the product with him and built the platform and the app in 21 days. Google rejected the first version (money-transfer apps must come from an organization), so I moved it to Innodite's verified organization account. Approved on November 1, live on Google Play.

    • Certificate pinning
    • Payloads encrypted on top of HTTPS
    • Requests signed with a secure-chip key, verified server-side
    • Root and tampering detection
    • Fingerprint login
